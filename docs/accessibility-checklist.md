# Accessibility checklist

- [ ] All controls have accessible names.
- [ ] Keyboard users can reach and operate navigation, search, cart and dialogs.
- [ ] Focus is visible and returns to the opener when dialogs close.
- [ ] Text and gold accents maintain sufficient contrast against dark backgrounds.
- [ ] Product images have meaningful alternative text.
- [ ] Form errors are announced and tied to their fields.
- [ ] Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- [ ] Layout remains usable at narrow mobile widths and browser zoom.
