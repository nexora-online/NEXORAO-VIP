# Public information routes

- `/impressum` — provider details to be completed before sales.
- `/datenschutz` — privacy notice requiring final review against deployed services.
- `/agb` — terms not yet active.
- `/widerruf` — withdrawal information to be completed before sales.
- `/versand` — shipping details pending confirmed supplier arrangements.
- `/kontakt` — support contact channel pending setup.
- `/zahlung` — payment methods pending provider activation.
- `/faq` — preview-phase customer questions.
- `/ueber-uns` — project overview.
- `/kollektionen` — planned categories.
- `/vip` — planned VIP area.

These pages intentionally do not invent legal identity, shipping prices, payment availability, or supplier commitments.
